Immediate Free Crisis Support (UK & Global)

You do not have to carry this alone. If you are in distress or having thoughts of ending your life, please connect right now. All lines below are 100% free, confidential, and run 24 hours a day.

Samaritans (UK & Ireland) — 24/7 Free
116 123

Free from any phone, will not appear on your itemised bill. A trained volunteer will listen without judgment.

Shout Crisis Text Line — 24/7 Silent Text
Text SHOUT to 85258

If speaking out loud feels impossible, text SHOUT for free confidential support on all UK networks.

Emergency Medical Care (UK)
999 / NHS 111

If your life is in immediate danger or you require medical intervention, call 999 or attend your nearest hospital A&E.
